The apartment

Space, light and a view that inspires

A corner apartment on the 11th floor, with connected spaces designed for family life. Its corner layout lets natural light flow through the home all day long.

The living area brings together the living room, dining room and open kitchen, while the balcony frames a wide view of the city and the mountains. The main bedroom has its own bathroom and a walk-in closet. Two more bedrooms, a study and a second bathroom make it comfortable for family life and working from home.
