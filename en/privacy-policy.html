Personal data processing policy

How we process the personal data you share with us, under Colombian Law 1581 of 2012.

1. Data controller

Leidy Johana Bolaños Rodríguez (Johana Bolaños, real estate advisor), based in Bogotá, Colombia. Email: contacto@johanabolanos.com. Phone and WhatsApp: +57 312 406 6324.

2. Data we collect

Name, phone, email and any information you share about the property or transaction you're interested in, including the values you enter in our calculators.

3. Purposes

  • Responding to your requests and sending the reports and simulations you ask for.
  • Contacting you by phone, WhatsApp or email about the real estate advice you requested.
  • Sending you information about properties and related services, if you authorize it. You can ask us to stop at any time.
  • Keeping records of clients and prospects in our customer management tool (CRM).

4. Your rights

Under Colombian Law 1581 of 2012 you may access, update and correct your data; request proof of your authorization; be informed of how we use it; file complaints with the Superintendency of Industry and Commerce; revoke your authorization and request deletion of your data when there is no legal or contractual duty to keep it; and access it free of charge.

5. How to exercise them

Write to contacto@johanabolanos.com with your name, your request and a way to reach you. Inquiries are answered within ten (10) business days and claims within fifteen (15) business days, as provided by Law 1581 of 2012.

6. Security and processors

We use technology providers (email and CRM) that act as data processors and may store information on servers outside Colombia. We take reasonable measures to protect your data and we do not sell or transfer it to third parties for commercial purposes.

7. Effective date

This policy is effective as of November 26, 2026. Your data will be kept as long as necessary for the purposes described or until you request its deletion.
