Services

Advice for selling and buying

Personal support from start to finish, so you can make one of life's biggest financial decisions with complete clarity.

If you're selling

  • Financial report with suggested price and net proceeds.
  • Professional presentation: photos, video and a dedicated property page.
  • Promotion on social media and portals, plus screening of buyers.
  • Support through negotiation, purchase agreement and deed signing.

If you're buying

  • Search based on your budget, area and lifestyle.
  • True total cost of the purchase before you make an offer.
  • Guidance on mortgages and paperwork.
  • Coordination with a lawyer and notary for a safe closing.

If you live abroad

  • Video-call tours and complete information on each option.
  • Viewings, paperwork and follow-up handled in Bogotá.
  • Guidance to buy or sell remotely, including power of attorney.
  • Service in Spanish and English.
